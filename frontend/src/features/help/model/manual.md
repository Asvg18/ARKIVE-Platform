# ARKIVE User Manual: Applicant Tracking and Evaluation System Guide

## Section 1: Introduction

ARKIVE is the online applicant tracking and evaluation system of the Manuel S. Enverga University Foundation (MSEUF) HR office. Applicants use it to find openings, apply, and follow their application through each hiring stage. HR staff use it to run the hiring process from job posting to onboarding, and evaluators use it to score interviews.

### 1.1 How to Use This Manual

- **Section 2, Getting Started:** account creation, sign-in, account settings, and notifications, for all users.
- **Sections 3, 4 and 5:** step-by-step guides for applicants, HR admins (including super admins), and evaluators. Read only the section for your role.
- **Section 6:** frequently asked questions and troubleshooting.
- **Section 7:** ARKIVE's terms and conditions.

Words in **bold** are buttons, links, or menu items exactly as they appear on screen. A path such as **Menu > Find Jobs** means: open the menu on the left side of the screen, then select Find Jobs. All dates and times in ARKIVE are in Philippine Time (PHT).

### 1.2 User Roles

Each account has one role. The role decides which portal you see after signing in and what you can do there.

| Role | Portal | What they do | How they get an account |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Applicant | Dashboard | Find jobs, apply, upload a resume, answer pre-screening questions, book exams and interview times, respond to offers | Signs up on their own |
| HR Admin | HR Dashboard | Create and publish job openings, set each job's hiring workflow, review candidates, schedule exams and interviews, record hiring decisions | Invited by a super admin |
| Super Admin | HR Dashboard | Everything an HR admin does, plus invite staff, deactivate accounts, and make other HR admins super admins | Invited or promoted by a super admin |
| Evaluator | Review Queue | Set available interview times, see only the interviews HR assigns them, score those interviews | Invited by a super admin |

### 1.3 How Hiring Works in ARKIVE

Every job has its own hiring workflow, chosen by HR. A workflow uses some or all of these stages, always in this order:

1. **Document Upload:** the applicant uploads a resume.
2. **Pre-Screening:** the applicant answers questions, and ARKIVE compares their profile with the job.
3. **Shortlisting:** HR picks who continues.
4. **Examination:** set by the HR.
5. **Interview Rounds:** each scored by an assigned evaluator.
6. **Final Decision:** HR hires or rejects, and a hire receives an offer.
7. **Onboarding:** the new hire receives a checklist.

ARKIVE's skill-matching never rejects an applicant by itself. A weak match is flagged for an HR staff member, who makes the decision.

## Section 2: Getting Started

This section applies to every user. Applicants sign up on their own; HR admins, super admins, and evaluators join through an emailed invitation.

### 2.1 System Requirements

- **Device:** a computer, tablet, or smartphone with an up-to-date browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. The HR and evaluator portals work best on a desktop screen.
- **Email address:** one you can open. ARKIVE sends verification links, password reset links, staff invitations, and notifications there.
- **Resume (applicants):** a PDF file no larger than 10 MB.
- **Profile photo (optional):** an image no larger than 2 MB.
- **ARKIVE web address:** the link provided by the MSEUF HR office.

### 2.2 Creating an Applicant Account

1. Open ARKIVE and select **Create an account** on the sign-in page.
2. Enter your first name, last name, and email address.
3. Create a password, then type it again to confirm it.
4. Select **Create account**. ARKIVE shows **Check your email** and sends you a verification link.

ARKIVE checks each field before creating your account:

| Field | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| First name and last name | Must start with a letter. Letters, including accented ones (Niño, Peña), spaces, hyphens, apostrophes, and periods are allowed, for example "Ma. Cristina" or "Dela Cruz-Santos." Up to 100 characters each. |
| Email | A valid address such as name@example.com. One account per email address. |
| Password | 8 to 72 characters. Cannot be the same as your email address. |
| Confirm password | Must match the password exactly. |

### 2.3 Verifying Your Email

1. Open the email from ARKIVE and select the verification link.
2. On the **Verify your email** page, select **Verify email**.
3. When you see **Your email is verified**, select **Go to sign in**.

The link expires after **24 hours**. If it has expired, try to sign in: ARKIVE shows "Please verify your email before signing in" and offers **Resend verification email**.

### 2.4 Accepting a Staff Invitation

HR admins, super admins, and evaluators cannot sign up on their own. A super admin invites them by email, and the invitation link works once, for **7 days**.

1. Open the invitation email and select its link. The page shows **Accept your invite** and your access level: HR Admin, Super Admin, or Evaluator.
2. Your email is already filled in. Enter your first name, last name, and password, and confirm the password. The same field rules apply as in section 2.2.
3. Select **Accept invite & create account**.

### 2.5 Signing In and Out

1. On **Sign in to your account**, enter your email and password.
2. Select **Sign in**. ARKIVE opens your portal: **Dashboard** for applicants, **HR Dashboard** for HR staff, or **Review Queue** for evaluators.

After **5** wrong attempts, sign-in pauses for up to **15 minutes**. A session ends after **12 hours** at most, and you must sign in again. To sign out, open your profile menu and go to **Account settings**, then select **Sign out**.

### 2.6 Forgotten Password

1. On the sign-in page, select **I forgot my password**.
2. Under **Reset your password**, enter your email and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and select the link. It lasts **1 hour** and works once.
4. Under **Choose a new password**, enter a password of at least 8 characters, confirm it, and select **Set new password**.

If the link has expired or was already used, request a new one.

### 2.7 Account Settings

Open your profile menu and select **Account settings**.

- **Profile:** change your name and, for staff, your job title and department. Select **Save changes**.
- **Profile photo:** select **Upload photo**, crop it, and save. Select **Remove photo** to go back to showing your initials.
- **Sign-in email:** select **Use a different address**, enter the new email, and select **Send confirmation link**. The change takes effect when you open the link sent to the new address, within **24 hours**. Select **Cancel change** to stop it.
- **Password:** select **Change password**, enter your current password and a new one twice, and select **Update password**. Changing it signs you out on every other device.
- **Closing your account:** applicants can delete their own account (see section 6). HR and evaluator accounts are closed by a super admin from **User Management**.

### 2.8 Notifications

ARKIVE notifies you when something on your application or work changes, such as a new stage, a booking, or an assigned interview. New notifications pop up briefly in the corner of the screen.

- Select the **Notifications** bell to see the latest ones, then **See all** for the full list.
- Open a notification to read it; opening it marks it read. Use **Mark as unread** to flag it again.
- Select **Archive** to move a notification out of your inbox. It stays in **Archived** and can still be searched.
- Use **Search notifications** to find one by subject, message, or sender.

## Section 3: Applicant Guide

As an applicant, you find a position, apply with your resume, and then move through the hiring stages HR set for that job. ARKIVE tells you what to do at each stage. Your menu has two items: **Dashboard** and **Find Jobs**.

### 3.1 Finding a Job

1. Select **Menu > Find Jobs**.
2. Type in **Search by title, skill or keyword**, or narrow the list with the **Department** and **Deadline** filters.
3. Sort the list by **Newest first**, **Closing soonest**, or **Title (A–Z)**.
4. Select a position to see **About the role**, **Qualifications**, the **Deadline**, and the **Hiring process**. The hiring process lists every stage the job runs through, so you know what to expect before you apply.
5. Select **Apply Now**.

Positions you have already applied to are marked **Applied** and link to your application. A position marked **Applications closed** no longer accepts applicants.

### 3.2 Tracking Your Applications

Select **Menu > Dashboard** to see **My Applications**. Each application shows its current stage and status. Select **Continue** to open an application and do its next step.

### 3.3 The Hiring Stages

Each job has its own workflow, so your application may skip some of the stages below. They always run in this order.

| Stage | What you do | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Document Upload | Upload your resume, check the details ARKIVE reads from it, then select **Review & Submit** | ARKIVE matches your skills to the job |
| Pre-Screening | Answer the job's questions. Questions marked (optional) can be left blank | You move on, or your application is held for HR review |
| Shortlisting | If HR shortlists you, confirm with **Yes, continue** or withdraw | You move to the next stage |
| Examination | Book an exam session | You take the exam; HR advances you afterward |
| Interview Round 1 to 6 | Pick one of the times HR sends | You attend; an evaluator scores the interview |
| Final Decision | Nothing; the screen shows **Under Final Consideration** | HR approves or declines |
| Offer | Select **Accept Offer** or **Decline Offer** | Accepting moves you to Onboarding |
| Onboarding | Tick off the onboarding checklist | HR contacts you about your start date |

### 3.4 Uploading Your Resume (Document Upload)

1. Under **Upload Your Resume**, select **Click to choose a file, or drag it here**. The file must be a **PDF** and no larger than **10 MB**.
2. Wait while ARKIVE shows **Reading your resume**. It fills in your contact details, education, work experience, skills, and certifications.
3. Under **Review Your Profile**, check each field and correct anything it read wrong. Enter skills and certifications separated by commas.
4. Select **Review & Submit**.

If ARKIVE shows "Couldn't read your resume," fill in the fields yourself and continue. To look back at what you sent later, open the application and select **Review My Submission**.

If your profile is not a strong match, you will see **Application Under Review**. **Your application has not been rejected.** It is held until an HR staff member reviews it and decides whether you continue. While you wait, ARKIVE suggests other open positions.

### 3.5 Pre-Screening

Answer each question in the box marked **Type your answer here**, then submit. ARKIVE compares your resume and answers with the job's requirements.

### 3.6 Booking an Exam

1. Pick a date. Only dates with a free seat can be selected.
2. Pick a time on that date, then select **Book This Session**.
3. Confirm with **Book Session**. Your booking takes effect right away.

You can switch to a different session until **24 hours before it starts**. If no date works, add an optional note and select **Tell HR**. If no sessions are listed yet, HR has not added any; you will be notified when they do. All times are in Philippine Time (PHT).

### 3.7 Scheduling an Interview

HR sends you a set of interview times. Pick a date, then a time, and select **Confirm Time**. For an online interview, the meeting link appears after you pick a time.

If none of the times work, select **None of these work**, add an optional note, and select **Send to HR**. Once you confirm a time, contact the HR office if you need to change it.

### 3.8 Responding to a Job Offer

You have **7 days** from the date of the offer to respond. The screen shows your exact deadline.

- Select **Accept Offer** to proceed to Onboarding.
- Select **Decline Offer** to turn the position down. Your application closes as withdrawn. **This cannot be undone**; you would have to apply again.

If you do not respond by the deadline, the offer lapses and your application closes.

### 3.9 Onboarding

Tick each step as you finish it. HR can see your progress, so you do not need to send anything back through ARKIVE.

- [ ] Sign and return the offer letter
- [ ] Submit pre-employment requirements
- [ ] Attend HR orientation
- [ ] Set up institutional email and accounts

### 3.10 Withdrawing an Application

1. Open the application from your **Dashboard**.
2. Select **Cancel Application**, then confirm with **Cancel Application**. Select **Keep Application** to back out.

A withdrawn application is closed for good, and you cannot take further steps on it. HR sees it as withdrawn, not rejected.

## Section 4: HR Admin Guide

As an HR admin, you publish job openings, set each job's hiring workflow, and move candidates through it until you hire or reject them. Your menu has: **HR Dashboard**, **Create Job Opening**, **HR Workflow**, **Job Openings**, **Workflow Builder**, and **Pre-Screening Questions**. Super admins also see **User Management** (section 4.9).

### 4.1 The HR Dashboard

**Menu > HR Dashboard** shows **Hiring at a glance** and **Waiting on You**, the candidates who need an action from you. It counts **Flagged** applications and candidates at **Final decision**, and lists every open job with its **Active Candidates**. A job past its deadline stays open but is hidden from applicants.

### 4.2 Creating a Job Opening

1. Select **Menu > Create Job Opening**.
2. **Name the role** and add the **Job Description**. Upload the job description PDF and ARKIVE reads it and drafts the fields for you, or write them yourself. Drafted fields are marked **AI drafted** and stay editable.
3. Set the **Department** and **Application deadline**.
4. Choose the **Hiring Workflow** (section 4.3).
5. Add the **Pre-Screening** questions (section 4.4).
6. On **Check and publish**, select **Publish job opening**, or save it as a draft to finish later.

A job can only be published when it has a title, department, deadline, description, workflow, and **at least one pre-screening question**. The **Before this can be published** list shows what is still missing. A draft does not need any of these.

### 4.3 Setting the Hiring Workflow

Select **Menu > Workflow Builder**, then pick an open job. Drag stages to reorder them, name each interview round and examination, and choose how long each interview runs. Select **Save Workflow**.

ARKIVE refuses to save a workflow that breaks these rules:

- **Document Upload** comes first.
- **Shortlisting** comes before any interview.
- **Final Decision** comes after the interviews.
- **Onboarding** comes last.

A workflow can have up to **4 examinations** and **6 interview rounds**. Also add the evaluators who may interview for this job to its **evaluator pool** here.

**Once a job has active candidates, its stage order is locked.** You can still rename stages, change interview lengths, and edit the evaluator pool; select **Save Names & Lengths**. The order unlocks when nobody is active in the pipeline.

### 4.4 Pre-Screening Questions

Select **Menu > Pre-Screening Questions**, pick a job, edit its questions, and select **Save Questions**. Applicants answer them in the order shown. Questions lock while the job has active candidates, so everyone in a pipeline answers the same set.

### 4.5 Working the Pipeline (HR Workflow)

Select **Menu > HR Workflow** and pick an opening. Switch between the **Board** and **List** views; both show the same candidates. Each candidate shows a **Match %** from Pre-Screening.

To advance a candidate, drag their card onto the next stage or select **Advance**. ARKIVE only allows a move to the next stage in the workflow; it rejects skipping ahead.

Open a candidate to see their **Candidate Profile**: resume details, documents, **Questions & Answers**, exam results, interviews, and the **Synthesis** summary of **Core Strengths** and **Areas of Concern**.

### 4.6 Flagged Candidates

When Pre-Screening finds a weak match, the application is **Flagged**, not rejected. It waits for you. On the Candidate Profile, choose one:

- **Approve to Continue** clears the flag, and the candidate moves to the next stage.
- **Reject Application** closes the application as rejected.

### 4.7 Exams and Interviews

**Shortlisting.** Record a **Shortlisting Decision**. A shortlisted candidate must confirm they still want to continue before they move on.

**Exam sessions.** Add sessions for each examination: pick the dates, the time windows, and the location. Applicants book a free seat themselves. You can also **Book a Session for Them** or **Move to Another Session**.

**Exam scores.** Exams are taken outside ARKIVE. Once marked, select **Enter Score**, enter a score from **0 to 100** and the date taken, and select **Save Score**.

**Interviews.**

1. Assign an evaluator from the job's evaluator pool.
2. Tick one or more times and select **Send Times**. For an online interview, a meeting link is required; for an in-person one, a location.
3. The applicant picks a time, or tells you none work. Select **Send New Times** to offer others.

You can also use **Set Schedule** to fix a time yourself. The interview then appears in that evaluator's **Review Queue** and no one else's.

### 4.8 Final Decision, Offer and Pre-201

The **Final Decision Board** ranks every candidate who has cleared all interview rounds. It is for information only; select **Download Excel** to export it.

To decide, open the candidate's profile and select **Record Decision**. Approving a hire sends the applicant an offer, which they have 7 days to accept. Use **Reject & Archive** to end an application at any stage. A closed application can be reopened with **Reopen**; the earlier decision stays on record.

After an applicant accepts, open their **Pre-Employment Records** (Pre-201 package). It gathers their contact details, letter of intent, shortlisting, exam and interview results in one place.

### 4.9 Super Admin: User Management

Only super admins see **Menu > User Management**.

**Inviting staff**

1. Select **Invite Staff**.
2. Enter the person's email and choose their access: **HR Admin**, **Super Admin**, or **Evaluator**.
3. Select **Send Invite**. They set their own password when they accept.

If ARKIVE cannot send email, select **Copy Link** and send the invitation link yourself. An invite can be **Pending**, **Accepted**, **Expired**, or **Revoked**; use **Resend** to send it again.

**Managing accounts**

- **Deactivate** switches an account off; **Reactivate** turns it back on. A deactivated evaluator cannot be given new interviews.
- **Make super admin** and **Remove super admin** change an HR admin's access. ARKIVE keeps **at least two super admins**, so one person being away cannot lock HR out.

## Section 5: Evaluator Guide

As an evaluator, you interview the candidates HR assigns to you and score each interview on the scorecard. You see only your own assigned interviews. Your menu has one item: **Review Queue**.

### 5.1 Your Review Queue

**Menu > Review Queue** lists your interviews under **Awaiting your evaluation** first, then the rest. Each shows a status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Awaiting Evaluation | The interview time is confirmed; the scorecard is open |
| Waiting for Schedule | The candidate is at this round but has not confirmed a time yet |
| Upcoming | Assigned to you, but the candidate has not reached this round yet |
| Evaluated | You have submitted the scorecard |

When nothing needs scoring, the queue shows **You're all caught up**.

### 5.2 Setting Your Availability

HR offers candidates interview times based on when you are free.

1. Open an interview and go to **My availability**.
2. Pick a date, a start time, and an end time, and add the range.
3. Select **Save availability**.

Time ranges on the same day cannot overlap. All times are in Philippine Time (PHT).

### 5.3 Before the Interview

Open the interview to see the candidate's **Resume** and the **Interview schedule**: the date and time, and either the location or the **Meeting link** for an online interview. If you can no longer make the time, request a new date and time and select **Send to HR**. The interview shows **Reschedule requested, waiting on HR** until HR responds.

### 5.4 Scoring the Interview

The scorecard is the **Applicant Preliminary Interview Evaluation Form (HRD-F-APIEF)**. It opens once the interview time is confirmed.

1. Under **Ratings**, rate every item from **1 to 5**: 5 Outstanding, 4 Very Good, 3 Good, 2 Improvement Needed, 1 Unsatisfactory. Items are grouped under Personal Background, Educational Qualifications, Work Background, and Job Expectations.
2. Under **Overall recommendation**, choose **Highly Recommended**, **Recommended**, or **Not Recommended**.
3. Under **Remarks**, write what stood out: the candidate's strengths and where they would need support.
4. Select **Submit evaluation**.

Your progress is saved **on this device only** until you submit, so finish on the same computer and browser. **Submitting locks the evaluation**; you cannot edit it afterward. HR sees your scores, recommendation, and remarks on the candidate's profile.

## Section 6: Frequently Asked Questions and Troubleshooting

Most problems come from an expired link, a file that is not a PDF, or a step that is waiting on someone else. If none of the answers below help, contact the MSEUF HR office.

### Signing In and Accounts

**My verification link has expired.** Verification links last 24 hours. Try to sign in; ARKIVE shows "Please verify your email before signing in" with a **Resend verification email** option.

**I forgot my password.** On the sign-in page, select the forgot password link and enter your email. The reset link lasts **1 hour**.

**ARKIVE says "Too many failed sign-in attempts."** After **5** wrong attempts, sign-in pauses for up to **15 minutes**. Wait, then try again, or reset your password.

**I was signed out while working.** For security, a session ends after **12 hours** at most. Sign in again; submitted work is saved.

**My staff invitation link does not work.** Invitations last **7 days** and work once. Ask a super admin to **Resend** it.

**How do I delete my account? (Applicants)** Go to your account settings and choose to delete your account. You must enter your password and type **DELETE**. Your open applications are withdrawn, and your resume and documents are permanently deleted. **This cannot be undone.** HR keeps a record that the applications happened, under "Deleted applicant" rather than your name.

### Applying

**My resume will not upload.** It must be a **PDF** no larger than **10 MB**. Save a Word file as PDF first.

**ARKIVE filled in my details wrong.** Correct the fields under **Review Your Profile** before you select **Review & Submit**. If it could not read the file at all, type the details yourself.

**My application says "Application Under Review."** Your profile was not a strong match in Pre-Screening, so an HR staff member will decide whether you continue. It has not been rejected.

**There are no exam sessions to book.** HR has not added any yet. You will be notified when they do. If none of the listed dates work, select **Tell HR**.

**Can I change my exam session?** Yes, until **24 hours before it starts**. After that, contact the HR office.

**Can I change my interview time?** Once you confirm a time, contact the HR office to change it.

**I missed the offer deadline.** Offers last 7 days. After that the offer lapses and the application closes; contact the HR office.

**Can I undo a withdrawal or a declined offer?** No. You must apply again while the position is still open.

### HR Admins

**I cannot publish a job.** Check the **Before this can be published** list. A common cause is missing pre-screening questions when the workflow includes Pre-Screening.

**I cannot reorder a workflow or edit its questions.** The job has active candidates, so the stage order and questions are locked. You can still rename stages, change interview lengths, and edit the evaluator pool.

**I cannot move a candidate to a later stage.** Candidates move one stage at a time, in workflow order.

**An evaluator is missing from the assign list.** Only evaluators in the job's evaluator pool can be assigned. Add them in **Workflow Builder**. A deactivated evaluator cannot be assigned.

**I do not see User Management.** Only super admins see it. Ask a super admin to invite staff for you.

### Evaluators

**The scorecard will not open.** It opens only once the candidate has reached your round and confirmed an interview time. Check the status in your **Review Queue**.

**I lost my unsubmitted scores.** Drafts are kept only on the computer and browser you used. Return to that device, or score the interview again.

**I need to change a submitted evaluation.** Submitted evaluations are locked. Contact the HR office.

## Section 7: Terms and Conditions

By creating an ARKIVE account or accepting a staff invitation, you agree to these terms.

### 7.1 Purpose

ARKIVE is used only to recruit and evaluate applicants for positions at Manuel S. Enverga University Foundation. It may not be used for any other purpose.

### 7.2 Your Account

- Give accurate information and keep it up to date.
- Keep your password private. You are responsible for everything done under your account.
- Each person may hold one account per email address. Staff accounts are created only by invitation from a super admin.
- Tell the HR office right away if you think someone else has used your account.

### 7.3 Information You Submit

- Applicants confirm that their resume, documents, and answers are true and complete. False or misleading information may disqualify an application at any stage.
- Upload only your own documents, as PDF files up to 10 MB.

### 7.4 Automated Pre-Screening

ARKIVE uses automated tools to read resumes and compare applicants' skills with each job's requirements. These tools assist HR; they do not make hiring decisions. **No applicant is rejected by the system alone.** A weak match is flagged for an HR staff member, who decides whether the application continues. The matching does not use age, race, or gender.

### 7.5 Privacy and Data Protection

MSEUF processes personal information in ARKIVE in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

- Your information is seen only by MSEUF HR staff and the evaluators assigned to your interviews.
- ARKIVE keeps a record of each stage change and each automated step, for accountability.
- Applicants may delete their account at any time from account settings. This withdraws open applications and permanently deletes the resume and documents. A record that the applications took place, with their stages and scores, is kept without the applicant's name.

### 7.6 Staff and Evaluator Responsibilities

- Use applicant information only to evaluate applicants, and do not copy or share it outside the hiring process.
- Evaluate each candidate fairly, on the scorecard criteria only.
- Submitted evaluations are final and cannot be edited.
- Super admins may deactivate any staff account at any time.

### 7.7 Hiring Decisions

All hiring decisions are made by MSEUF. An application, an interview, or the stage reached in ARKIVE does not guarantee employment. An offer that is not accepted within 7 days lapses.

### 7.8 Changes to These Terms

MSEUF may update these terms. Continuing to use ARKIVE after a change means you accept the updated terms.

### 7.9 Contact

For questions about these terms or your personal information, contact the MSEUF HR office.

## Conclusion

ARKIVE keeps hiring at MSEUF clear for everyone involved: applicants always see where their application stands, HR staff run each job's workflow in one place, and evaluators score only the interviews assigned to them. Follow the section for your role, and check Section 6 if something does not work as expected. For further help, contact the MSEUF HR office.
